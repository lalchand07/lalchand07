## What I do

I build software that takes manual work off a team's plate: AI agents and workflow automation, booking and operations systems, and the integrations that connect them. Travel CRMs I built are in production, and I have also built a multi-module hospital records system, an education ERP, and a certificate verification platform for an Albanian certification body.

Before going independent, I led a team on EPADS, Pakistan's federal e-procurement platform, delivered by Emenac under a government contract. Tenders, evaluations, contracts and payment gateway integration. It runs statutory processes and cannot quietly be wrong, and most of my habits around testing and audit trails come from there.

I deliver client work through **[Codic Systems](https://codicsystems.com)**, the software company I founded in Islamabad in August 2026.

<details>
<summary><b>How I work</b></summary>

<br>

- **Written specification before any code**, including an explicit list of what is *not* included
- **Architecture decision records**, one page per significant choice: what I decided, what I considered, why
- **Tests where being wrong costs money**, meaning anything touching payments or bookings
- **A human review pass on every AI-generated module** for security boundaries, error handling, and anything the model may have invented
- **Handover pack** with README, environment setup, deployment steps, credentials, architecture diagram, known limitations

Fixed price against a defined outcome. I do not bill hours: when tooling cuts a twenty hour job to five, hourly billing punishes me for getting faster.
